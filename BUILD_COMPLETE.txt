                            PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

NetPlan is a complete, production-ready web application for learning IPv4
subnetting with real-world FLSM (Fixed Length Subnet Masking) and VLSM
(Variable Length Subnet Masking) calculators.

LOCATION: c:\workspace personal\Netplan\
